Support

Need help with Drift? We're here for you.

Common questions:

  • Restore purchases: Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" to restore any previously purchased premium plans.
  • Cancel subscription: Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store (App Store or Google Play). Open your store settings to manage or cancel.
  • App not working correctly: Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, reinstall the app — your purchases will be restored automatically.
  • Audio not playing: Make sure your device is not on silent mode and the volume is turned up.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 19, 2026

Drift ("the App") is a breathing and meditation app. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or location.

All session data (breathing patterns, visual art, preferences) is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads to free users. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General usage and interaction data

You can learn more about how Google uses data in its Privacy Policy and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Premium users see no ads, and no ad-related data is collected for them.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The App does not collect or have access to your payment information.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Apple App Store — in-app purchases (iOS)
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (Android)

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or need support, contact us at: nalhamzy@gmail.com
